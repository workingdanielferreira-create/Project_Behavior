Save skin

Writes a NEW character folder beside the original (frames repainted, character.json and FX file copied and renamed, plus painter.json so you can come back and repaint). The original is never touched. Press F5 in the game to load it.
Paint outside the tool
Export this frame (with an anatomy guide layer) to paint in any editor, then import the paint back as a transparent PNG of the same size.
How it works. Rig Forge characters store every frame's joints (hip, chest, neck, head, shoulders, elbows, hands, hips, knees, feet, weapon tips) in character.json. Each body pixel belongs to the nearest bone (near limbs and weapons in front of the torso, far limbs behind it). For every frame, each body pixel is traced back along its bone (position along the bone, distance across it) into the frame(s) you painted, and takes that paint. Joints blend between the two bones that meet there. Characters without joint data (e.g. ronin, nobody) can't be painted yet.
