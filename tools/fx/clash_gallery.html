Clash Explosion Gallery

The clash explosions from laser/clashfx.py, ported to the browser canvas tick for tick (16 ms): eleven baselines, then the eight pair clashes the clash interaction plays when two kinds of FX meet (marked “Pair”). Side A’s colour fills the left half of each explosion, side B’s the right, with a white-hot core where they meet. In the game, press F6 to fire them at the cursor.
